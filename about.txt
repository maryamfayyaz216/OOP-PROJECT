-------------------- ABOUT THE GAME --------------------

A wealthy business owner hosted a party at their cottage.
During the party, a locker hidden behind a bookshelf in
the Study Room was robbed. The locker contained valuable
documents and assets. Eight people were present in the
cottage that night and one of the seven suspects is guilty.

The owner's Personal Assistant has hired you as a private
detective to investigate the case.

-------------------- YOUR MISSION -----------------------

1. Visit seven locations in the cottage. Each location
   gives one letter tile and its board position.
2. Interview eight people, including the victim. Each
   person gives one letter tile and its board position.
3. Place every letter on the virtual Scrabble board.
4. Form two words from the collected letters.
5. Use the words to identify the culprit's role.
6. Accuse the correct suspect before turns or credibility
   run out.

-------------------- HOW TO BEGIN -----------------------

Select [4] Rules from the Main Menu to read the game rules.
Select [1] New Game to start your investigation.

Good luck, Detective.
